About

Grassroots cricket, reinvented at home.

Interest in cricket is at a high — record participation, new audiences and fresh investment. Infinite Nets turns that momentum into something players can actually use: repeatable, measurable practice, any day of the year.

The problem

Practice is hard to come by.

Good facilities are scarce, winters are wet and dark, and finding someone to bowl at the right standard is tough. Club nets can offer too little time with the bat, and for beginners the gear and the public setting are barriers in themselves.

The solution

A free-to-download VR net.

Infinite Nets gives players high-quality bowling on demand, year-round, at home — with gamified drills, unlimited repetitions and technique feedback for every level. A grassroots-first platform that clubs, schools and partners can get behind.
